Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up or login. Every feature works as soon as you open the app.
Where does the data come from?
Public sources only: City of Los Angeles Open Data (data.lacity.org) for 311 requests, USGS for earthquakes, and the NOAA National Weather Service for alerts.
How often does the data update?
The apps reload data every 60 seconds, and the refresh button reloads it instantly. The City of Los Angeles publishes new 311 requests roughly every 20 minutes, so the newest requests shown may be up to about 20 minutes old.
The app shows an error or no data.
Check that your iPhone is connected to the internet, then tap the refresh button. If a public data source is temporarily unavailable, the app shows that source's status on its Settings tab. If the problem continues, email us.
Do the apps collect my personal data?
No. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
