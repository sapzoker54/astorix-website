1. Overview & Scope

Gensis.AI Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the ASTORIX platform, a suite of AI-powered civic and network intelligence iOS applications. This Privacy Policy explains how our apps handle information when you use them.

Our apps are designed with a privacy-first architecture: they display live public data from government and open APIs, and they do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use them.

Core principle: ASTORIX apps show you real-time public data. They do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to our servers.

2. Apps Covered

This policy applies to all apps published under the ASTORIX brand on the Apple App Store:

  • HOPE-LA Community infrastructure monitoring for Los Angeles (Bundle ID: ai.astorix.hopela)
  • CityInfra Infrastructure intelligence: 311 data, earthquakes, weather (Bundle ID: ai.astorix.cityinfra)
  • Signal Real-time network intelligence and ISP monitoring (Bundle ID: ai.astorix.signal)
  • Signal Pro Enterprise network + seismic + weather risk intelligence (Bundle ID: ai.astorix.signalpro)

Our apps collect and process the following types of data locally on your device only:

5. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account credentials
  • No location (GPS coordinates) — we do not request location permissions
  • No device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, serial number)
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No advertising identifiers — we run no ads
  • No payment information
  • No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • No biometric data
  • No crash reports sent to our servers (we use no analytics SDK)

We do not maintain user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for. Your use of the app is entirely anonymous to us.

6. How Data Is Used

All data fetched by our apps is used solely to display information to you, on your device, in real time. Specifically:

  • Public API data (311, USGS, NWS) is fetched, processed in-app, and displayed. It is not stored after the session or sent anywhere beyond the original API source.
  • Your IP address (Signal / Signal Pro) is sent to ipapi.co to retrieve your ISP name, which is displayed on screen. We do not log, store, or transmit this data to any ASTORIX server.
  • Network performance measurements are computed entirely on your device and displayed within the app session only.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes.

We do not have servers that receive data from your device (other than the standard HTTPS request required to deliver the app). We have no database of user information to share.

The only outbound requests your device makes are to the public APIs listed in Section 4, which are the same requests you could make in any web browser.

8. Data Retention

All data processed by ASTORIX apps is session-only. When you close the app, all in-memory data (network scan history, fetched API results) is discarded. Nothing is written to persistent storage on your device beyond what iOS itself may cache for performance.

We retain no data on our servers because we receive no data from you.

9. Security

All API requests made by our apps use HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). We enforce Apple's App Transport Security (ATS) requirements.

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data at risk of a breach on our end.

10. Children's Privacy

Our apps are rated 4+ on the Apple App Store and contain no objectionable content. They are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), and by design we collect no personal information from any user of any age.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the address in Section 13 and we will take appropriate action.

11. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, most data-subject rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) are fulfilled automatically — there is no data about you to access, correct, delete, or export.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Given that we collect no personal information as defined by the CCPA, there is nothing to disclose, sell, or delete. We do not sell personal information.

If you are in the European Union or UK, you have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR. We do not process personal data as a controller. The third-party APIs in Section 4 may process your IP address as a data processor when you make requests to them.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of our apps after changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
