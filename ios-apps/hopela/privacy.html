Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Gensis.AI Inc. ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ASTORIX™ HOPE-LA mobile application and web platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Microsoft, we receive your name, email address, and organizational affiliation from Microsoft Entra ID. We do not store your Microsoft password.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymized usage data including feature interactions, session duration, and search queries within the platform to improve the Service.
  • Device Information: We collect device type, operating system version, and app version for technical support and compatibility purposes.
  • Business Data You Enter: Any contract information, business data, or queries you enter into the platform are processed to provide AI analysis. This data is associated with your organizational account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To provide AI-powered contract intelligence and business analysis
  • To improve platform features and accuracy of AI models
  • To send you service-related communications (not marketing)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Microsoft Azure: Our cloud infrastructure provider (data processed in the United States)
  • OpenAI / AI providers: Queries sent for AI analysis are processed by AI service providers under data processing agreements
  • Law enforcement: Only when required by applicable law

4. Data Retention

We retain account information for the duration of your active account. Usage data is retained for up to 24 months for analytics purposes. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and Microsoft Entra ID for authentication. Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@astorix.ai.

7. Children's Privacy

ASTORIX™ HOPE-LA is a business platform not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

The app uses Microsoft Entra ID for authentication. Microsoft's privacy policy applies to your Microsoft account: privacy.microsoft.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date above and, where appropriate, through in-app notification.
